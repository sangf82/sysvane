What we heard

The brief

A tray-first Windows app that replaces vendor suites for monitoring, displays, RGB, fans and the AIO LCD. It runs near zero RAM and only shows a window when asked.

quietexacttechnicallight

Success: reads at 16 px in the tray on light and dark taskbars, works in one colour, and does not look like a gaming-brand mark.

Concept A

Crate

A crate as an isometric cube: the machine's devices in one box, with the lid lit.

  • Three faces and one lit lid: one owner, many devices
  • Survives one colour: the gaps keep the cube readable in black or white
  • Ink tile app icon stays visible on light and dark taskbars
  • Emerald is the only colour, as in DESIGN.md
